# M1 manual checks

What the product owner checks by hand before signing off M1 ([roadmap](../roadmap.md)). Judge feel and smoothness in a **Release** build (`cmake --preset rel && cmake --build --preset rel`); Debug is 3–5× slower (ADR 0015).

Mark each item with the date and any notes; failures become issues or open questions.

## Devices (plugins and in the DAW)

- [ ] Saturator: sound and feel (curves, Color, Quality)
- [ ] EQ: sound and feel (adding, dragging and editing bands; analyser)
- [ ] Subtractive Synth: sound and feel (defaults sound good immediately; envelopes, filter, mod slots, voice modes)
- [ ] Sampler: sound and feel (drop a file, markers, loop crossfade, one-shot, transposition)
- [ ] All four load in a Linux host (VST3, CLAP or LV2) and their state survives save/reload

## DAW shell (C1)

Run `build/rel/app/fd-daw_artefacts/Release/Segno` (add `--software` to compare the software renderer).

- [ ] The layout matches the UI principles: transport bar, browser, arrangement, inspector, detail panel
- [ ] Panels open and close smoothly: the toolbar buttons, Ctrl+Alt+B / I / D, focus mode Ctrl+Alt+F
- [ ] Below 1440 px wide the browser becomes a rail and the inspector closes; the rail button brings the browser back
- [ ] Shortcuts: Space plays/stops, Home returns to the start, Ctrl+T adds a track, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo and redo
- [ ] Audio settings (Ctrl+, or the sliders button) open as a popover and close by clicking away
- [ ] Tooltips show each button's shortcut

## Arrangement (C2)

- [ ] Wheel scrolls tracks; Shift+wheel and horizontal swipes scroll in time; Ctrl+wheel and pinch zoom at the mouse
- [ ] Dragging in the ruler: vertical zooms around the press point, horizontal scrolls; a click moves the playhead
- [ ] =, − and Ctrl+0 zoom smoothly (animated); zooming always keeps the point under the mouse or the playhead in place
- [ ] The grid adapts as you zoom (sixteenths up to many bars) and bar numbers never crowd
- [ ] Dropping audio files on a track places them at the snapped position (Alt: free); dropping below the last track creates tracks
- [ ] Smooth (display refresh rate) while zooming and scrolling, on the laptop panel and the external display

## One kind of track (ADR 0019)

- [ ] Audio and MIDI clips sit on the same track; an audio clip plays through a track that also has a synth (the synth adds, never mutes)
- [ ] Dropping or moving a clip over others cuts them (trimmed, split or removed) instead of overlapping; one Ctrl+Z restores both
- [ ] Dropping a `.mid` file on a track without an instrument adds the Subtractive Synth and says so; the header shows the instrument's name

## Clip editing (C4)

- [ ] Click selects a clip; Shift or Ctrl adds or removes; dragging on empty space draws a selection box
- [ ] Dragging a clip moves it with snapping (Alt: free), also onto other tracks; several selected clips move together
- [ ] Dragging a clip's left or right edge trims it; trims stop at the start and end of the audio file
- [ ] Ctrl+E splits at the playhead, Ctrl+D duplicates after the selection, Delete removes; Ctrl+A selects all
- [ ] Every gesture undoes in one step (Ctrl+Z) and redoes (Ctrl+Shift+Z)
- [ ] The inspector shows the selected clip's position and length

## Projects (C8)

- [ ] Ctrl+S saves a project (asks for a name the first time); the title shows the name, with * after changes
- [ ] Ctrl+O reopens it: tracks, clips, devices and their settings, loaded samples, tempo, loop, zoom and panels come back
- [ ] New, Open and quitting with unsaved changes ask to save; Cancel keeps you where you were
- [ ] `Segno path/to/song.segno` opens the project at start

## Instrument tracks and MIDI (C7)

- [ ] Adding an instrument to a track (+ in its chain) and selecting the track lets you play it from a MIDI keyboard with no extra setup
- [ ] Selecting another track stops the MIDI keyboard playing the previous one; plugging a keyboard in while the app runs works
- [ ] Ctrl+Shift+K turns the computer keyboard into a MIDI keyboard (A W S E D F ... play notes, Z/X octave, C/V velocity); notes stop when keys are released
- [ ] The Sampler on an instrument track plays a dropped sample from the keyboard
- [ ] Dropping a `.mid` file shows its notes in a clip, and it plays through the track's instrument

## Device chain (C6)

- [ ] Selecting a track shows its devices in the detail panel; + adds our devices; the chain scrolls when wide
- [ ] Ctrl+Alt+P scans your plugins (VST3, LV2); they then appear under + > Plugins by manufacturer
- [ ] A third-party plugin shows as a card; "Open editor" opens its window; a native device and a plugin work on one track
- [ ] Dragging a device's header reorders the chain; the power toggle bypasses; clicking a header then Delete removes it; undo restores
- [ ] Playing audio through the chain sounds right (no glitches while adding or reordering)

## Transport (C5)

- [ ] Space plays and stops; Home stops and returns to the start; a click in the ruler moves the playhead
- [ ] Dragging in the strip above the bar numbers draws a loop and turns looping on; dragging it moves it, its edges resize it, a click toggles it; Ctrl+L toggles looping
- [ ] Looping playback is seamless at the loop boundary
- [ ] Metronome (Ctrl+M or the button) clicks in time, accenting bars
- [ ] Tempo and time signature change by dragging or typing; Ctrl+Z undoes a change; the grid follows
- [ ] While playing, the view follows the playhead smoothly (Ctrl+Shift+F turns it off)

## Waveforms (C3)

- [ ] Dropped files show their waveform within a moment (long files too), in a lighter tint of the clip colour
- [ ] Waveforms stay steady while zooming and look crisp once the zoom stops (no visible popping)
- [ ] `--stats` shows paint times; `--benchmark` reports under 8 ms p95 on both displays
