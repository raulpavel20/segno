# M3 manual checks

What the product owner checks by hand before signing off M3 ([plan](../milestones/M3-workflow-and-space.md)). Judge feel in a Release build.

## Phaser & Flanger (B3)

- [ ] On a guitar, synth or drum loop: the default is a gentle two-notch phaser sweeping slowly, about as loud as the module switched off
- [ ] Phaser, Flanger and Doubler: the first section takes the mode's name; switching while it plays never clicks, and switching back finds each mode's settings as they were
- [ ] Phaser: Notches from 1 to 12 deepens it; Spread bunches the notches or spaces them; Center moves them; Feedback makes the peaks between them ring
- [ ] Flanger: Time sets the comb (shorter is higher); Amount sweeps it down towards zero; Through Zero (the plugin host reports 1 ms latency) passes the dry sound, and with Invert and feedback it folds in on itself: the tape-flange sound
- [ ] Doubler: a second take Time behind, moving a little either way; with Offset 180° each side's take moves the other way
- [ ] The display: the notches (or the comb) move with the sweep, the left side's over the right's, inside a band showing the range; its line reads the range ("through zero" when it passes the dry sound); in Doubler it draws the take's movement
- [ ] Drag in the display: sideways moves Center or Time (right is higher), up and down widens the sweep; Shift drags finely
- [ ] Rate in Hz, or note values with Sync, locked to the song; the shapes (Sine to Drift) sound as named, Square and Random without clicks; Spin makes the sides drift in and out of step
- [ ] ENVELOPE on: playing harder pushes the sweep (Amount either way), rising within Attack and falling back within Release
- [ ] Warmth darkens and softens; Low Cut keeps a bass's lows solid at any mix and in every mode
- [ ] Notches and Spread dim outside the Phaser, Through Zero outside the Flanger, ENVELOPE while off, Freq while Low Cut is off
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Classic phaser to Slap doubler: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; synced, it follows the host's tempo and position

## Chorus (B2; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a synth pad or a clean guitar: the default is a gentle, wide two-voice chorus, about as loud as the module switched off; the meters agree
- [ ] Chorus, Ensemble and Vibrato: Chorus thickens, Ensemble is lusher with a quick shimmer (the string-machine sound), Vibrato bends the pitch with no dry sound; switching mode while it plays never clicks
- [ ] The voices display: a line per voice over two cycles, the left side's in the accent colour over the right's; its line reads the pitch swing in cents and the delay range; a marker moves with the movement
- [ ] Drag in the display: sideways changes Rate, up and down Depth; Shift drags finely; with Sync on, sideways steps through the note values
- [ ] Rate turns in Hz; with Sync on it turns note values (8 bars … 1/32, faster to the right) and stays locked to the song: play from different places, the movement lands on the beat the same way
- [ ] Shape from 0 to 100 %: smooth to the triangle's two steady pitches (try the Siren preset); Offset 0° makes it mono (L and R the same), 180° the widest
- [ ] Delay: short is metallic, long is a doubled, wider sound; changing it glides the pitch, never clicks
- [ ] Width from 0 % (mono voices) to 200 %; Warmth darkens and softens, more so with feedback; Feedback and Invert give a resonant, hollow ring
- [ ] Low Cut on: a bass keeps its low end solid and centred at any Dry/Wet and in Vibrato; Freq moves where the effect starts; off, the whole sound is chorused
- [ ] Vibrato dims Delay, Width, Feedback, Invert and Dry/Wet; Low Cut off dims Freq
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Classic chorus to Metallic: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; synced, it follows the host's tempo and position

## Clip loops (C10, ADR 0048; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] Drop a one-bar drum loop on a track; drag the upper part of its right edge (the loop cursor) out to 16 bars: it repeats, a notch in the title strip and a line through the body at every bar, the loop mark at the end of the title strip; the tag says "16 bars · 16 loops" while you drag; it plays seamlessly, no clicks at the seams
- [ ] Drag a notch: the loop's length follows (snapped; Alt for free), every lap reflows, "Loop 2 beats" while you drag; the first lap stays where it is
- [ ] Drag a looped clip's left edge to the left: earlier laps appear, the beat stays on the grid where it was
- [ ] Clip > Loop clips and the clip's right-click Loop clips switch it off (the repeats go, it ends at its first lap) and on (it loops what it shows; nothing moves); Ctrl+Z brings the repeats back
- [ ] Inspector, CLIP: Loop On; Loop start "1.2.1" and Loop length "1 bar" dragged and typed ("6 beats", "1 bar 2 beats", for audio "1.5 s"); nothing touches, at the narrowest inspector
- [ ] Double-click the audio clip: the clip view shows its file's waveform, the loop's part bright, the rest faded; drag on the strip above the ruler to loop a different beat; drag its edges and body; click it to switch Loop off and on; play: the playhead runs round the loop
- [ ] A MIDI clip: the same brace above the piano roll; draw notes past the loop's end: the loop grows to that bar, the clip doesn't; notes outside the loop are faded and silent
- [ ] Split a looped clip inside a lap and on a seam, duplicate it, bounce it in place: each plays as before; crossfade out of it into the next clip: no gap, no click
- [ ] Change the tempo: a MIDI loop follows it; an audio loop stays its length in seconds (warping comes in M4)
- [ ] Record MIDI with Overdub over a looped MIDI clip: the pass makes its own clip; the loop keeps its notes
- [ ] A clip with takes (bounced, or recorded over): Loop clips is dimmed and says why
- [ ] Save, reopen: every loop as it was; an assistant can loop, unloop and set loops (`loop_clips`) and trim a looped clip past its file

## The window: Maximise and Restore (fix; signed off 2026-10-12)

On Linux (GNOME on Wayland, then KDE if you have it), with our title bar:
- [ ] Maximise, then click around the browser (open and close Instruments, a folder, star a module, click a preset): the window stays maximised, with no outline, and the button keeps showing Restore
- [ ] Restore returns the window to the size and place it had before; so does a double-click on the title bar, and the title bar's right-click menu
- [ ] Drag a maximised window's title bar down: it restores under the pointer and follows it
- [ ] Super+Up and dragging the window to the top of the screen maximise it, and the button shows Restore; Restore then works
- [ ] F11 from a window that isn't maximised fills the screen, and F11 again goes back; from a maximised window, F11 fills the screen and F11 again comes back maximised

## The editing grid (C9, ADR 0047; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] The arrangement shows "● Snap  1/16 ⌄" in the corner above the track headers; zoom in and out: the step follows (adaptive), and Snap and the step never touch
- [ ] Click the step: Adaptive ▸ Narrowest … Widest, Fixed ▸ 8 bars … 1/32, Triplets, Snap to grid; Narrow and Wide each change the step at the same zoom; Fixed 1/4 stays 1/4 at every zoom, its lines thinned when you zoom far out
- [ ] Open a MIDI clip: the clip view's header shows the piano roll's own Snap and step beside Quantise; set Fixed 1/16 and draw notes by double-click: every one is a sixteenth, zoomed in or out
- [ ] Triplets: 1/8T draws and snaps three to a beat; in 3/4 the lines start again at every bar
- [ ] Options ▸ Narrower grid (Ctrl+1), Wider grid (Ctrl+2), Triplet grid (Ctrl+3), Snap to grid (Ctrl+4), Fixed grid (Ctrl+5) change the piano roll's grid after you've worked on notes, the arrangement's otherwise; the other editor's never changes; Fixed starts at the step showing, so nothing moves
- [ ] Right-click an empty spot in the arrangement's lanes, or the notes: a Grid submenu with the same items
- [ ] Save, reopen: both grids as you left them; Ctrl+Z never undoes a grid change; a project from before keeps its Snap in both
- [ ] Assistants: get_grid and set_grid read and set each editor's grid

## Delay (B1; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a vocal or a guitar: the default is a dotted-eighth echo at the song's tempo; change the tempo: the echoes follow, bending like tape (Time Change: Repitch) or crossfading (Fade), never clicking
- [ ] The repeats display shows each echo where it falls against the beat, at its level; drag the first left bar: the time follows, snapping to note values while synced, freely in milliseconds with Sync off; Shift drags finely; with Link off, the lower lane drags the right side
- [ ] Sync off: Left and Right turn in milliseconds; on: they step through the note values (1/16, 1/8T, 1/8D…), shortest to longest; Right dims while Link is on, Swing while Sync is off
- [ ] Ping-pong bounces the echoes from left to right; Mid-side on a stereo source echoes the centre and the sides at their own times
- [ ] Feedback near 100 % with Drive: the echoes thicken and stay bounded; Freeze holds what's there, forever, and lets nothing new in; off again, it fades away as before
- [ ] The filter curve shows what each pass takes away; drag near its low or high edge to move that cutoff; the FILTER power dims it and lets the repeats through whole
- [ ] Tape darkens and saturates each pass; Analog is darker and softer; Wobble wavers like a worn machine; Mod Time shimmers, Mod Filter sweeps the repeats' tone
- [ ] Ducking: the echoes wait while you play and come back in the gaps, all the way within Duck Release
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Slapback to Build to infinity: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; the echoes follow the host's tempo

## Presets (A1; signed off 2026-10-11)

- [ ] Every module's header shows ‹ the preset's name ⌄ › and an A button, right of the module's name; nothing touches, on the narrowest module (Utility) too
- [ ] A new module reads "Default settings"; move a knob: a dot follows the name; move it back (or undo): the dot goes
- [ ] Click the name: the list opens under it (or above, where there's no room below), the search field ready to type
- [ ] The arrow keys load each preset as they move, so you hear each one; Enter keeps the one you're on; Ctrl+Z after it undoes the whole browse in one step
- [ ] Escape closes the list and puts back what was there before it opened
- [ ] Typing filters by name, tags and description; the tag buttons and Favourites narrow the list; a star makes a preset a favourite, in the plugins too
- [ ] Save as…: the form opens in place with the name selected; a name you already use asks to Replace; the new preset shows in the list, and the module reads its name
- [ ] Right-click one of yours in the list: Rename… edits it in place; Delete moves it to the system's trash; Show in folder opens its folder
- [ ] The presets are files in Documents/Segno/Presets/<Module>; copy one in by hand: it shows the next time the list opens
- [ ] ‹ and › step through the presets (the list as last filtered), wrapping round
- [ ] A switches to B (one undo step): edit B, switch back to A and forth; Copy A to B (right-click the name, or Module) makes them the same; save, reopen: A and B are still there
- [ ] Use as the default for a new Compressor: the next Compressor added starts from it ("Default"); Clear the default: back to its own
- [ ] A parameter with automation keeps following its lane after a preset loads; the others take the preset's values
- [ ] The Module menu lists every preset command for the selected module; dimmed with no module selected
- [ ] In another DAW (REAPER, Bitwig, Ardour): the plugin's header has the same bar and list; its window grows while the list is open; a preset saved there shows in Segno, and the other way round; the host's program list shows Default settings and the factory presets
- [ ] Assistants: list_presets, load_preset, save_preset and compare do what the header does

## Browser (C2; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] The browser on the left: Favourites, Instruments, Audio effects, then Places (Project once saved, Segno, your folders) and Add folder…; folders stay open as you left them, after a relaunch too
- [ ] Open the Subtractive Synth: Yours (once you've saved one), then Bass, Brass, Drums… with their counts; open the Compressor: its presets A to Z
- [ ] Double-click a module or a preset: it goes on the selected track (an instrument in place of the track's own, with Undo in the toast); with a module selected, a preset of its kind loads into it
- [ ] Drag a module, a preset or a sample: onto a track, into the module chain (a line shows where; a preset over a module of its kind, or a sample over the Sampler, outlines it), onto a mixer strip, below the tracks for a new track; each one Ctrl+Z
- [ ] Click a sample: it plays at once; with the song playing, a loop that fits the tempo plays in time with the bar; the strip shows its waveform, length, tempo and rate; its volume drags
- [ ] Click an instrument preset: a short phrase plays; an effect's preset is silent; Preview off (the header's switch, or View) stops selecting from playing
- [ ] Ctrl+F: type "bass": results grouped (Presets, Samples…), "All 16 presets" shows the rest, the tag buttons narrow; Escape gives the tree back as it was
- [ ] Stars: a sample, a module, a preset (the same star as its header's list); Favourites lists them
- [ ] Add folder… a folder of your samples: its files show at once and are found by search; move the folder away: the place greys with Locate… and Remove from places; Settings > Library lists your places too
- [ ] File > Collect files and save, with clips from outside the project: they're copied into its Samples; move the project folder to another machine: everything plays
- [ ] File > Remove unused files…: lists only files nothing uses and moves them to the trash after asking
- [ ] At a narrow window: the browser is a rail; each icon opens it over the arrangement at that part; a click elsewhere or a drag out closes it
- [ ] Nothing touches, at standard and compact widths; long names end in an ellipsis with the whole name as a tooltip

## Settings (C1; signed off 2026-10-11)

- [ ] Options > Settings… (Ctrl+,) and the top bar's settings button show Settings in the centre; the browser and inspector stay, the detail panel hides; ✕, Escape, Settings… again or Mixer view close them, back to the view you came from
- [ ] The search narrows the page as you type, marks what matched, dims the sections left empty; "nothing like this" says No setting matches; Changed only shows only what you've changed
- [ ] Set up your interface from scratch: driver, output and input, channels, sample rate and buffer (each with its latency); Play the test tone; the Load line counts dropouts
- [ ] Change the buffer while a song plays: the audio stops for a moment and playing goes on
- [ ] Linux: with JACK or PipeWire's JACK running, JACK is in the driver list; a first launch (no Settings.xml in ~/.config/Segno) picks it
- [ ] Turn a MIDI input off: it's ignored everywhere; Computer keyboard on: Segno opens with it playing
- [ ] Recording: FLAC, 16-bit; record a pass: the file is a FLAC; Count-in follows the Record button's menu and the other way round; the Record button's menu "Recording settings…" opens the page at Recording
- [ ] Each choice marks its default ("(default)"); change one: the section's title row shows Reset; Reset puts the whole section back and hides itself
- [ ] Library: Move… the Segno folder elsewhere: your presets come along; New projects: Save as… starts there
- [ ] Plugins: Add… a folder with a plugin, Scan: it's found; a plugin that crashes a scan shows under Skipped with Try again
- [ ] Export: change the defaults; a new project's export panel starts from them; an export makes its settings the next new project's
- [ ] Keys: click a shortcut, press new keys: the menus, tooltips and palette show them at once; a key another action has asks "… is X's." with Replace and Cancel; Backspace clears one; Reset all shortcuts appears while any differs
- [ ] Interface size 125 %: everything grows at once; Automatic: back
- [ ] The command palette lists "Settings: Buffer size" and the rest; choosing one opens the page at its row, marked for a moment
- [ ] The Assistants popover's "All assistant settings…" opens the page at Assistants
- [ ] Quit and reopen: everything is as you left it (~/.config/Segno/settings.json; app.json and assistants.json are gone into it)
- [ ] Narrow window (compact width): nothing touches; long action names in Keys fit beside their shortcuts

## Factory presets (B11; signed off 2026-10-11, the Sampler's to come)

Listen through each set in the DAW (the arrow keys in the list load as they go). For each: does the name say what you hear, does it sound good, is anything missing or redundant? Say what to change; Claude changes it.

- [ ] Utility (5): Mono check, Invert polarity, Pad -6 dB, Wide, Remove DC
- [ ] Spectrum (6): Mixing, Flat, Mid and side check, Find resonances, Fast, Slow and smooth
- [ ] EQ (18), on a vocal, drums, bass, a guitar or keys, and a mix
- [ ] Compressor (15), on the same; Sidechain duck with a kick under Sidechain; De-ess on a vocal with Listen
- [ ] Gate (12), on drum microphones with bleed, a vocal, a guitar; Trance gate with a rhythmic sidechain
- [ ] Limiter (12), on a mix and on busses
- [ ] Saturator (15): each about as loud as the dry sound, so you hear the colour, not the level
- [ ] Subtractive Synth (35): basses low, leads and keys in the middle, pads with chords, drums around C1 to C3
