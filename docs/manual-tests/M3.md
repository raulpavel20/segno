# M3 manual checks

What the product owner checks by hand before signing off M3 ([plan](../milestones/M3-workflow-and-space.md)). Judge feel in a Release build.

## The song row and its sections (C3)

- [ ] The master's row sits under the ruler and stays there while the tracks scroll; its header still selects the master (its modules below, its mix in the inspector); its bottom edge resizes it
- [ ] Every track shows as a thin line in its colour where it plays, the tracks scrolled out of sight too
- [ ] A drag across empty time makes a section, snapped to the grid; its name is A, B, C…; a double-click on the name renames it there, and right-click > Name offers the usual names
- [ ] Dragging a section's edge moves only its mark; dragging its body moves it with all its music: it follows the pointer, a bracket shows where it lands (between sections, or in empty time), and the rest of the song makes room; Ctrl+drag copies it
- [ ] Ctrl+C on a section, the playhead moved, Ctrl+V: a copy with its music there, the rest later (inside a section: at its nearer edge); change the original, paste again: the copy is as it was; Ctrl+X, then Ctrl+V elsewhere moves it; each one Ctrl+Z
- [ ] Sections look like brackets over their span, not like clips; a selected one is tinted; two touching ones keep a gap between their ticks; names cut short with an ellipsis
- [ ] What you hear moves exactly: a moved section sounds the same in its new place, with no clicks at the edges, its automation too; a clip across an edge is split there
- [ ] Ctrl+D copies a section after itself, Delete removes it with its music (the gap closes), Remove section mark keeps the music; each one Ctrl+Z
- [ ] Double-click a section's body to loop it; Alt+← and Alt+→ jump between sections; the Song menu has every command
- [ ] The inspector's SECTION: its name, colour, start and length (the marks)
- [ ] An assistant can list, mark, move, copy, insert and delete sections

## EQ stage 2, step 2a (B8)

- [ ] A dynamic band: turn on DYNAMICS for a bell on a vocal's harsh range; it cuts only when that range gets loud, its curve moving live, its range handle (the hollow node) dragged up and down to set how far; the trigger meter shows the level against the threshold
- [ ] Auto Threshold: on a steady part nothing moves; louder moments in the band's range are turned down. Off: the threshold knob sets it, as a fixed level
- [ ] Sidechain: choose the kick in the header; on a bass, a dynamic bell at 60 Hz with Trigger Sidechain dips only while the kick plays
- [ ] The masking display: the kick's curve over the bass's spectrum, the clash shaded; hover it and "Make room for Kick" adds that band in one click, one Ctrl+Z
- [ ] Auto Gain: boosting or cutting broadly keeps the level about the same; the correction it applies shows under it
- [ ] Listen plays only the selected band's range (Alt+drag a node does too); Delta plays only what the dynamic bands take away; neither is saved with the project
- [ ] Every new preset: Boom control, Bass under the kick (with a kick chosen under Sidechain)
- [ ] Undo and redo of any module's knob: heard and shown at once
- [ ] Old projects: their EQs sound as before

## EQ stage 2, step 2b (B8)

- [ ] Mode (Processing): its menu names each choice's delay, and it opens every time; Linear phase shows "+107 ms" (or so) at the header's right end, its tooltip saying what it means for playing live; the toast says it once for the project; the tracks stay in time with the EQ on one of them
- [ ] Quality (Resolution) shows only while the EQ adds delay; it changes the delay as its menu says (Low, Medium, High)
- [ ] Linear phase sounds like Zero latency on a mix with gentle moves, and keeps a kick's attack through a steep low cut
- [ ] A spectral band: on a harsh guitar, a ringing vocal or a resonant room, switch Spectral on a wide bell over the problem; only what sticks out comes down, live on the curve; Detail high finds narrow rings, low broad build-ups; Delta plays what it takes away
- [ ] Spectral with Trigger Sidechain: a bass bus ducks only at the kick's frequencies while it plays
- [ ] Spectral at Zero latency: the other bands keep their sound, all of it delayed together; switching Spectral restarts playback once, no click afterwards
- [ ] Listen and Delta switch in time with what you hear in linear phase
- [ ] Every new preset: De-ess (on a vocal), Tame harshness (a bus), Resonance smoother (a mix), Linear-phase master
- [ ] An assistant sees the delay in `get_module` (latencyMs)
- [ ] Old projects: their EQs sound as before, with no delay

## Loudness Meter (B7)

- [ ] On the master, playing a finished song from the start: Integrated, Range and True peak match a reference meter's within 0.1 (LUFS, LU, dBTP), reset before; Short-term and Momentary move with it
- [ ] Song: loop the chorus several times; Integrated stays the song's, played once. Change a part and play just that part again: Integrated follows. Stop: the bars still move with what you play in, Integrated holds, and the history says it counts while the song plays
- [ ] Running: everything counts as it plays, from Reset; Pause holds Integrated and Range while the bars move; Reset clears
- [ ] The history: the song's loudness from start to end, parts not played empty, the playhead, the target's band, red ticks where true peaks pass the ceiling; hover reads a moment
- [ ] Integrated turns green within 1 LU of the target and amber above it; True peak red above the ceiling; Range dimmed for its first minute
- [ ] Normalised: a master louder than the target reads how far a service turns it down; a quieter one, how far up the peaks allow
- [ ] Correlation reads +1 for mono, near 0 for a wide pad, below 0 (amber) when a side is flipped; its line holds the lowest. Balance leans the way a mix leans
- [ ] Units LU and Scale +18 redraw the bars, the history and the readouts; Target and Peak Ceiling move the lines
- [ ] Save, close and reopen the project: the song's reading and its history are still there
- [ ] A new meter reads for streaming (−14 LUFS, −1 dBTP, Song); every factory preset: Quieter streaming and podcasts, Loud master, Broadcast Europe, Broadcast US
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; Song follows the host's transport
- [ ] An assistant reads the meter (`measure_module`), and `render` reports the loudness range

## Filter (B6; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a drum loop, a pad or a bass: the default is a 24 dB low pass at 2 kHz; Freq sweeps it smoothly, with no zipper, and the display's curve and handle follow
- [ ] Each circuit sounds like its name with Resonance up and some Drive: Clean exact, Warm thick and smooth, Acid rubbery and squelchy, Gritty raw; Warm and Acid whistle near 100 % and stop when the sound does
- [ ] Each type: the passes and Notch; Morph from low to band to high pass; DJ flat at the centre, a low pass to the left and a high pass to the right; Comb ringing at a pitch (Morph from peaks to notches); Vowel speaking a, e, i, o, u (Morph from a deep voice to a small one); Notch + LP; Resampling's grit following Freq
- [ ] Switching type, circuit or slope while it plays never clicks; what a type doesn't use dims
- [ ] The display: drag sideways for Freq (Sweep, Vowel), up and down for Resonance; the output's spectrum behind; its line reads where the filter is, or how far the modulation takes it
- [ ] The LFO: a wobble in time with the song (Sync, the note values), Phase against the beat, the shapes, Offset or Spin moving the sides apart
- [ ] The envelope: a wah that opens as you play harder; with a kick chosen under Sidechain, a pad that closes on every kick (Env Amount negative)
- [ ] Drive warms and grits the sound without jumping in level; Clip keeps loud resonance under 0 dBFS; Dry/Wet and Output
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Slow sweep to Notch sweep: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel, and the sidechain through the host's routing

## Reverb (B5, core; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a vocal, a snare or a pad: the default is a natural hall at 30 %, about as loud as the module switched off
- [ ] Room, Hall, Plate and Space each sound like their name: Room close and quick, Hall big and slow to build, Plate dense and bright with no early echoes, Space vast and moving; switching dips and comes back without a click
- [ ] Decay sets how long it rings, Size how big the space is, independently: a small room with a long decay, a big hall with a short one; turning Size glides, never clicks
- [ ] The decay display shows how long each frequency rings; drag the lows, the middle or the highs up and down, and the crossovers sideways; its line reads the decay and the space's size (not for Plate)
- [ ] Low Decay over 100 % gives a warm, rumbling tail; High Decay under 100 % a tail that darkens as it fades
- [ ] Predelay holds the reverb back; Diffusion low gives separate echoes, high a smooth wash; Early brings the first reflections forward (dims in Plate); Motion makes the tail lusher; Width from mono to extra wide
- [ ] Freeze holds the tail forever and lets nothing new in; off again, it fades as set
- [ ] The FILTER curve and power: the Delay's look and drag; lows and highs cut going in
- [ ] Ducking: the reverb waits while you sing and swells in the gaps
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Small room to Long plate: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel

## Auto Pan & Tremolo (B4; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a pad, a guitar or a drum loop: the default is a quarter-note pan at the song's tempo, moving half way to each side, as loud as the module switched off
- [ ] Panning and Tremolo: the first section takes the mode's name; switching while it plays never clicks
- [ ] Panning: a mono sound moves across, a stereo one keeps its image as it moves; at full Amount it reaches each side without dipping in loudness
- [ ] Tremolo: the level pulses, to silence at full Amount; Offset 180° pulses the sides in turn, Spin makes them drift in and out of step
- [ ] The seven waves sound as named; Square and Random chop cleanly, without clicks; Edge makes any wave choppier (positive) or softer (negative); Invert turns it upside down
- [ ] Synced, the movement stays on the beat wherever you start the song; Phase moves the cycle against the beat (a tremolo's dip on the off-beat)
- [ ] Attack: each new note comes through clean and the movement fades in behind it, over Attack
- [ ] Harmonic: the lows and highs pulse in turn; Vintage: the dips come sooner and recover slower, a little warmer
- [ ] Low Cut on: a kick and a bass stay centred and steady while the rest pans or pulses
- [ ] The display: the movement over two cycles (each side's level, or the place between L and R), a marker where it is now; its line reads the depth ("dips to -6.0 dB") or the swing ("50L–50R") and the rate in Hz; drag sideways for Rate, up and down for Amount
- [ ] STEREO & TONE dims in Panning, Phase while Sync is off, Freq while Low Cut is off
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Slow pan to Swell after the hit: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel ("Segno Auto Pan & Tremolo"); synced, it follows the host's tempo and position

## Phaser & Flanger (B3; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a guitar, synth or drum loop: the default is a gentle two-notch phaser sweeping slowly, about as loud as the module switched off
- [ ] Phaser, Flanger and Doubler: the first section takes the mode's name; switching while it plays never clicks, and switching back finds each mode's settings as they were
- [ ] Phaser: Notches from 1 to 12 deepens it; Spread bunches the notches or spaces them; Center moves them; Feedback makes the peaks between them ring
- [ ] Flanger: Time sets the comb (shorter is higher); Amount sweeps it down towards zero; Through Zero (the plugin host reports 1 ms latency) passes the dry sound, and with Invert and feedback it folds in on itself: the tape-flange sound
- [ ] Doubler: a second take Time behind, moving a little either way; with Offset 180° each side's take moves the other way
- [ ] The display: the notches (or the comb) move with the sweep, the left side's over the right's, inside a band showing the range; its line reads the range ("through zero" when it passes the dry sound); in Doubler it draws the take's movement
- [ ] Drag in the display: sideways moves Center or Time (right is higher), up and down widens the sweep; Shift drags finely
- [ ] Rate in Hz, or note values with Sync, locked to the song; the shapes (Sine to Drift) sound as named, Square and Random without clicks; Spin makes the sides drift in and out of step
- [ ] ENVELOPE on: playing harder pushes the sweep (Amount either way), rising within Attack and falling back within Release
- [ ] Warmth darkens and softens; Low Cut keeps a bass's lows solid at any mix and in every mode
- [ ] Notches and Spread dim outside the Phaser, Through Zero outside the Flanger, ENVELOPE while off, Freq while Low Cut is off
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Classic phaser to Slap doubler: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; synced, it follows the host's tempo and position

## Chorus (B2; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a synth pad or a clean guitar: the default is a gentle, wide two-voice chorus, about as loud as the module switched off; the meters agree
- [ ] Chorus, Ensemble and Vibrato: Chorus thickens, Ensemble is lusher with a quick shimmer (the string-machine sound), Vibrato bends the pitch with no dry sound; switching mode while it plays never clicks
- [ ] The voices display: a line per voice over two cycles, the left side's in the accent colour over the right's; its line reads the pitch swing in cents and the delay range; a marker moves with the movement
- [ ] Drag in the display: sideways changes Rate, up and down Depth; Shift drags finely; with Sync on, sideways steps through the note values
- [ ] Rate turns in Hz; with Sync on it turns note values (8 bars … 1/32, faster to the right) and stays locked to the song: play from different places, the movement lands on the beat the same way
- [ ] Shape from 0 to 100 %: smooth to the triangle's two steady pitches (try the Siren preset); Offset 0° makes it mono (L and R the same), 180° the widest
- [ ] Delay: short is metallic, long is a doubled, wider sound; changing it glides the pitch, never clicks
- [ ] Width from 0 % (mono voices) to 200 %; Warmth darkens and softens, more so with feedback; Feedback and Invert give a resonant, hollow ring
- [ ] Low Cut on: a bass keeps its low end solid and centred at any Dry/Wet and in Vibrato; Freq moves where the effect starts; off, the whole sound is chorused
- [ ] Vibrato dims Delay, Width, Feedback, Invert and Dry/Wet; Low Cut off dims Freq
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Classic chorus to Metallic: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; synced, it follows the host's tempo and position

## Clip loops (C10, ADR 0048; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] Drop a one-bar drum loop on a track; drag the upper part of its right edge (the loop cursor) out to 16 bars: it repeats, a notch in the title strip and a line through the body at every bar, the loop mark at the end of the title strip; the tag says "16 bars · 16 loops" while you drag; it plays seamlessly, no clicks at the seams
- [ ] Drag a notch: the loop's length follows (snapped; Alt for free), every lap reflows, "Loop 2 beats" while you drag; the first lap stays where it is
- [ ] Drag a looped clip's left edge to the left: earlier laps appear, the beat stays on the grid where it was
- [ ] Clip > Loop clips and the clip's right-click Loop clips switch it off (the repeats go, it ends at its first lap) and on (it loops what it shows; nothing moves); Ctrl+Z brings the repeats back
- [ ] Inspector, CLIP: Loop On; Loop start "1.2.1" and Loop length "1 bar" dragged and typed ("6 beats", "1 bar 2 beats", for audio "1.5 s"); nothing touches, at the narrowest inspector
- [ ] Double-click the audio clip: the clip view shows its file's waveform, the loop's part bright, the rest faded; drag on the strip above the ruler to loop a different beat; drag its edges and body; click it to switch Loop off and on; play: the playhead runs round the loop
- [ ] A MIDI clip: the same brace above the piano roll; draw notes past the loop's end: the loop grows to that bar, the clip doesn't; notes outside the loop are faded and silent
- [ ] Split a looped clip inside a lap and on a seam, duplicate it, bounce it in place: each plays as before; crossfade out of it into the next clip: no gap, no click
- [ ] Change the tempo: a MIDI loop follows it; an audio loop stays its length in seconds (warping comes in M4)
- [ ] Record MIDI with Overdub over a looped MIDI clip: the pass makes its own clip; the loop keeps its notes
- [ ] A clip with takes (bounced, or recorded over): Loop clips is dimmed and says why
- [ ] Save, reopen: every loop as it was; an assistant can loop, unloop and set loops (`loop_clips`) and trim a looped clip past its file

## Delay (B1; signed off 2026-10-12)

- [ ] On a vocal or a guitar: the default is a dotted-eighth echo at the song's tempo; change the tempo: the echoes follow, bending like tape (Time Change: Repitch) or crossfading (Fade), never clicking
- [ ] The repeats display shows each echo where it falls against the beat, at its level; drag the first left bar: the time follows, snapping to note values while synced, freely in milliseconds with Sync off; Shift drags finely; with Link off, the lower lane drags the right side
- [ ] Sync off: Left and Right turn in milliseconds; on: they step through the note values (1/16, 1/8T, 1/8D…), shortest to longest; Right dims while Link is on, Swing while Sync is off
- [ ] Ping-pong bounces the echoes from left to right; Mid-side on a stereo source echoes the centre and the sides at their own times
- [ ] Feedback near 100 % with Drive: the echoes thicken and stay bounded; Freeze holds what's there, forever, and lets nothing new in; off again, it fades away as before
- [ ] The filter curve shows what each pass takes away; drag near its low or high edge to move that cutoff; the FILTER power dims it and lets the repeats through whole
- [ ] Tape darkens and saturates each pass; Analog is darker and softer; Wobble wavers like a worn machine; Mod Time shimmers, Mod Filter sweeps the repeats' tone
- [ ] Ducking: the echoes wait while you play and come back in the gaps, all the way within Duck Release
- [ ] Every factory preset, from Slapback to Build to infinity: each sounds like its name and description
- [ ] In a plugin host: the same panel; the echoes follow the host's tempo

## Factory presets (B11; signed off 2026-10-11, the Sampler's to come)

Listen through each set in the DAW (the arrow keys in the list load as they go). For each: does the name say what you hear, does it sound good, is anything missing or redundant? Say what to change; Claude changes it.

- [ ] Utility (5): Mono check, Invert polarity, Pad -6 dB, Wide, Remove DC
- [ ] Spectrum (6): Mixing, Flat, Mid and side check, Find resonances, Fast, Slow and smooth
- [ ] EQ (24), on a vocal, drums, bass, a guitar or keys, and a mix
- [ ] Compressor (15), on the same; Sidechain duck with a kick under Sidechain; De-ess on a vocal with Listen
- [ ] Gate (12), on drum microphones with bleed, a vocal, a guitar; Trance gate with a rhythmic sidechain
- [ ] Limiter (12), on a mix and on busses
- [ ] Saturator (15): each about as loud as the dry sound, so you hear the colour, not the level
- [ ] Subtractive Synth (35): basses low, leads and keys in the middle, pads with chords, drums around C1 to C3
